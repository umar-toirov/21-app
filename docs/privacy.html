Habit Zone Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026. Habit Zone is made by ILM HUB.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and full name, and a password (handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase; we never see it in plain text).
  • Your activity: the challenges you join, the tasks you complete, your points, streaks and progress.
  • Groups: the groups you join, your group tasks and progress, and the chat messages and announcements you post. Other members of the same group can see these.
  • Settings: your theme, sound and reminder preferences.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, and we do not show ads.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, track your challenges, calculate points and rankings, and show your group's progress. Points and names appear on leaderboards that other users can see.

Reminders

Daily reminders are scheduled on your phone. They are local notifications and are not sent from our servers. You can turn them off in Settings.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. Our service providers (Supabase for sign-in and database, Render for hosting) process data only to provide the service.

Security and retention

Data is sent over HTTPS. We keep your data while your account is active.

Delete your account and data

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Your account is deactivated immediately and your data is deleted after 30 days. You can also ask us to delete it by emailing us at the address below from your account email.

Children

Habit Zone is not directed at children under 13.
